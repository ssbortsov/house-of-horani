Designed for the theatrical extravagance of La Grande Soirée, a collision of tropical neon brightness and raw, shadowy timber, Oud Passion defines the high-impact energy of black-tie nightlife. Formulated at 32% Extrait de Parfum, it creates an unforgettable, room-filling trail that refuses to fade.

The fragrance explodes with an intoxicating burst of tart passion fruit, sun-drenched dried fruits, and golden saffron. This luminous contrast plunges directly into an opulent heart of crimson rose, patchouli, velvety white flowers, and rugged leather.

100 ml / 3.4 fl oz

Spray once or twice onto pulse points — the neck, the wrists, behind the ears — from 15–20 cm. At 32% concentration a little goes a long way: do not rub the wrists together, let the scent settle on its own. It also lasts beautifully on hair and clothing; test delicate fabrics first.
