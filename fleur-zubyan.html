Designed for the theatrical extravagance of La Grande Soirée, Precious Hibiscus is an exercise in pure olfactory maximalism. Bottled at an uncompromising 32% concentration, this composition commands the room from the first second, projecting an aura of velvet, smoke, and crimson opulence.

Velvet, smoke and crimson rose. Tart cassis and pink pepper flash open, orris and violet bloom at the heart, and dark leather with ancient oud anchors the drydown. A scent that commands the room from the very first second.

Top
Cassis, Pink Pepper, Saffron, Cinnamon
Heart
Rose, Orris, Violet, Geranium
Base
Oud, Leather, Vanilla, Musk, Patchouli, Vetiver
Olfactive family
Floral – Leather – Oud
Concentration
Extrait de Parfum, 32%
Volume
100 ml / 3.4 fl oz

Spray once or twice onto pulse points — the neck, the wrists, behind the ears — from 15–20 cm. At 32% concentration a little goes a long way: do not rub the wrists together, let the scent settle on its own. It also lasts beautifully on hair and clothing; test delicate fabrics first.
